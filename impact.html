Team sustainability and recruitment

All of us love STEM, and we believe that education in that field is essential. We believe STEM careers are the future. This is why we make it a priority to get more people interested and involved in STEM. Robotics is such a great way to do that with other people. We try to publicize our team within our school, as well as in our community and feeder schools, such as Indian Hills and Westwood View. These places allow us to show our work to younger kids and inspire passion in them.
