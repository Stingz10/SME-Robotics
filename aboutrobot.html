Bot breakdown

How our robot is built and why each system matters

Drivetrain

The drivetrain gives the robot its movement and agility. It allows the team to navigate the field quickly and make sharp turns under pressure.

Intake System

The intake collects game pieces and guides them into the robot's scoring mechanism with consistent positioning and control.

Scoring Mechanism

This subsystem is responsible for placing game pieces accurately, helping the team earn points through repeatable and efficient actions.
